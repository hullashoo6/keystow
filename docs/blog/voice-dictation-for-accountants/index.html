Voice Dictation for Accountants: Workpapers and Client Emails in Busy Season

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

Busy season is a writing season

Every return, audit and close produces text: workpaper notes, review comments, client emails asking for documents, explanations of adjustments. The technical work is the hard part, but the documentation is what eats the evenings. Most accountants think through a position faster than they can type it.

Dictation lets you write the note while the reasoning is in your head, not an hour later from memory.

What accountants dictate

  • Workpaper notes. The "why" behind a position: what you saw, what you concluded, what you checked. Speaking it right after the analysis keeps the reasoning intact.
  • Review comments. Clear questions for the preparer, written as you would say them across the desk.
  • Client emails. Document requests and deadline reminders, personalized instead of templated, because dictating thirty of them is faster than typing five.
  • Procedure narratives. Describing what was done, in plain language, while doing it.

Accuracy habits that matter here

Numbers, account names and references deserve a second look: read the figures before you send or file. Dictation handles the prose quickly; your review keeps the numbers exact.

A note on firm policy

KeyStow processes your voice on your own computer and keeps no audio recording or transcript history, which removes the cloud from the equation. That does not by itself make any tool compliant with your firm's IT policy or professional obligations. Check what your firm permits before installing anything on a work machine.

How KeyStow fits

KeyStow is an offline voice dictation app for Windows and Mac. Hold Right Ctrl on Windows (Right Option on a Mac), speak, release, and the text is pasted where your cursor is. Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, and dictation works without internet too.
