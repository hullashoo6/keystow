The Best Wispr Flow Alternatives in 2026 (Offline and One-Time Payment Options)

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

Wispr Flow is one of the most popular AI dictation apps. It turns speech into text in any app, on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android. But it is not the right fit for everyone. Its Pro plan costs $15 per month, or $12 per month billed annually. And Wispr's own privacy page says transcription always happens in the cloud, so your voice leaves your computer every time you dictate.

If you want dictation that works offline, keeps your voice on your own computer, or costs a one-time payment instead of a subscription, these are the best Wispr Flow alternatives in 2026.

KeyStow: $5 once, fully offline dictation for Windows and Mac

KeyStow is an offline voice dictation application for Windows and Mac. It runs OpenAI's Whisper speech recognition models locally, on your own hardware. Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, and dictation works without internet too. KeyStow does not keep an audio recording or transcript history.

Using it is simple: hold a key to talk, speak, and the text is typed where your cursor is. The activation key is Right Ctrl by default, and you can change it to Caps Lock, Right Shift, Right Alt or a custom key. KeyStow sits in the system tray, starts automatically when the computer turns on, and lets you pick from several Whisper model sizes to balance speed and accuracy. A simple dashboard shows how many words you have dictated.

KeyStow costs $5 once, for lifetime access including updates. The source code is open under the MIT license. Installers are available for Windows and Apple Silicon Mac.

Superwhisper: local models free, AI extras paid

Superwhisper's free plan gives you unlimited dictation with local Whisper models, and its own docs say that on the free plan nothing you dictate leaves your device. The Pro plan adds cloud models, AI processing and file transcription, at $8.49 per month, $84.99 per year, or $249.99 for a lifetime license. One license covers Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android.

Handy: free and open source

Handy is a free, open source speech-to-text app. Press a shortcut, speak, release, and your words appear in the text field you are typing into. Transcription happens on your own computer, without sending anything to the cloud. Handy works on Windows, macOS and Linux. It is a minimal, single-purpose tool, so there is less to configure and fewer extras.

Which Wispr Flow alternative should you pick?

  • Pick KeyStow if you want offline dictation on Windows or Mac for a single $5 payment, with nothing sent to the cloud.
  • Pick Handy if you want something completely free and open source, and you are happy with a minimal tool.
  • Pick Superwhisper if you want a free local option with paid AI extras on top, or you need dictation on your iPhone too.
  • Stay with Wispr Flow if you want one account covering Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android with cloud features, and a monthly or annual plan is fine.

Every app on this list turns speech into text well. The real choice is where your voice goes and how you pay: a subscription with cloud processing, or a local app you buy once.
