Murmur turns your voice into text, anywhere on Windows. Hold a key, speak naturally, release - polished words appear right where your cursor is.

Free & open source · Pay once if you love it · No account needed

Install in one click

Download the installer, run it, and Murmur sits quietly in your tray. No Python, no setup wizard maze.

02

Hold and talk

Hold Right Ctrl, say what you mean, let go. In email, chat, docs - anywhere you can type, you can now speak.

03

Stay private

Transcription runs entirely on your own machine. Your voice never leaves your device. No cloud, no subscription.

Powered by OpenAI's Whisper, running 100% locally. Your audio is never uploaded, sold, or stored anywhere but your PC.
