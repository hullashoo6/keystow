How to Set Up KeyStow on Windows or Mac

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

What you need

  • A 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC, or a Mac with macOS 14 or later.
  • A microphone (the built-in one is fine).
  • Five dollars, once. No account, no subscription.

Step 1: Buy, download and install

Buy KeyStow on hullashoo6.github.io/keystow. After payment you get the download links for both installers. On Windows, run KeyStowSetup.exe. On a Mac, open KeyStowMac.dmg and drag KeyStow into Applications. The base speech model is inside the app, so there is no second download.

The installers are not signed with a paid certificate, so Windows and macOS each ask you to confirm KeyStow the first time; follow the steps that come with your download.

Step 2: Allow the microphone (and, on a Mac, Accessibility)

Windows: there is no pop-up. If nothing is heard, open Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone and make sure microphone access and Let desktop apps access your microphone are on.

Mac: KeyStow needs two permissions under System Settings > Privacy & Security. Microphone: macOS asks the first time you dictate. Accessibility, for the shortcut and for pasting: KeyStow asks when it starts; switch it on, then quit and reopen KeyStow.

Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud. Dictation works without internet too.

Step 3: Learn the one shortcut

Hold Right Ctrl on Windows, or Right Option on a Mac, speak, and let go. KeyStow turns your speech into text and pastes it where your cursor is. You can pick a different key in the KeyStow window or from its tray (menu bar) icon. Nothing listens until you hold the key.

Step 4: Test it once

1. Click into any text box: Notepad, TextEdit, an email draft.

2. Hold your shortcut and say a short sentence.

3. Let go. Your sentence appears at the cursor.

If it does, you are set. Read what you dictated before you send it: speech recognition can be wrong.

Keeping it running

KeyStow starts when you sign in (you can switch that off from its tray or menu bar icon). Closing the KeyStow window quits it. Minimise the window instead to keep dictation available.

What you do not have to do

  • Create an account or sign in.
  • Pay again. $5 once, lifetime access including updates.
  • Worry about your recordings. KeyStow keeps no audio recording or transcript history.
  • Stay online. Dictation works without internet.
