02

Focus, hold, speak

Hold Right Ctrl while speaking, then release to stop. Keep your destination field focused while it processes. Existing custom settings may retain toggle mode. Review the transcript before sending.

03

Understand the limits

Recognition can make mistakes. Paste may be blocked by some apps or secure fields. Real microphone, shortcut and insertion checks are tracked separately from builds.

Speech recognition runs locally with faster-whisper. Current app code does not intentionally save recordings, upload audio or send telemetry. Model downloads use the network. Settings, word counts and diagnostic logs stay in your user folder; text passes through your clipboard.
