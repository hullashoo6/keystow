Superwhisper vs KeyStow: Two Local Dictation Apps Compared

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

If you have decided you want dictation that runs on your own computer instead of the cloud, two names come up quickly: Superwhisper and KeyStow. Both transcribe locally with Whisper models. They differ on price, extras and philosophy. Here is the honest comparison, with sources at the end.

Side by side

Facts checked against both products' own pages on November 24, 2026.

Where Superwhisper is stronger

Superwhisper has been around longer, and it shows in the extras: custom vocabulary and text replacements on the free plan, iPhone and Android apps, and a Pro tier with cloud models, AI processing of your text, file transcription and meeting transcription on macOS. If you want your dictation app to also rewrite, summarize or process meetings, Superwhisper is built for that. Its free plan is also genuinely unlimited for local dictation, which is remarkable value at $0.

Where KeyStow is stronger

KeyStow is deliberately simple, and it is the only one of the two that is open source (MIT), so you can read exactly what it does with your audio. It does one job: hold a key, speak, get text at your cursor, on Windows or Mac. No account, no modes to learn, no upsell. And if you want to pay once and be done, $5 lifetime is 50 times cheaper than Superwhisper's $249.99 lifetime license. Superwhisper's own docs note its free plan covers local dictation, but every Pro feature (cloud models, AI processing, file transcription) sits behind the paid tiers.

Which should you pick?

  • Pick KeyStow if you want private hold-to-talk dictation on Windows or Mac, open source you can verify, for a single $5 payment.
  • Pick Superwhisper free if you want unlimited local dictation at no cost, or you need iPhone/Android coverage.
  • Pick Superwhisper Pro if AI rewriting, file transcription or meeting notes are worth $8.49 a month or $249.99 once to you.

Both apps keep your voice on your computer for local dictation. The choice is about extras, platforms and how you prefer to pay.
