Voice Dictation for Small Business Owners: Every Hat, Less Typing

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

The paperwork never gets its own hour

Running a small business means writing all day without ever calling it writing: quotes, invoices, order updates, vendor questions, review responses, the social post that has been "tomorrow" for a week. None of it is hard. All of it is slow when typing is the toll every message pays.

Dictation shrinks the toll. A quote email that took ten minutes of typing takes two minutes of talking, and it usually reads better, because you write like you answer the phone.

What owners dictate

  • Quotes and estimates. Speak the scope the way you explained it to the customer, then tidy the numbers.
  • Vendor and supplier emails. Clear, complete, sent today instead of someday.
  • Review responses. Thank-you replies and calm responses to unhappy customers, drafted while the details are fresh. Reading them aloud first keeps the tone right.
  • Job notes and instructions. What was done, what is next, what the team needs to know in the morning.
  • The marketing that keeps slipping. One dictated paragraph a day is a blog post by Friday.

Why a $5 tool fits a small business

Small businesses drown in small subscriptions. KeyStow is deliberately not one: it costs $5 once, with no subscription and no account. It is an offline voice dictation app for Windows and Apple Silicon Mac: hold Right Ctrl on Windows (Right Option on a Mac), speak, release, and the text is pasted where your cursor is, in any app you already use. Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, and dictation works without internet too. KeyStow does not keep an audio recording or transcript history, and the source code is open under the MIT license.
