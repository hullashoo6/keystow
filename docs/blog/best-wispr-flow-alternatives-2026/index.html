Wispr Flow Alternatives in 2026: KeyStow vs Superwhisper

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

Wispr Flow is one of the most popular AI dictation apps. It turns speech into text in any app, on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android. But it is not the right fit for everyone. Its Pro plan costs $15 per month, or $12 per month billed annually. And Wispr's own privacy page says transcription always happens in the cloud, so your voice leaves your computer every time you dictate.

If you want dictation that works offline, keeps your voice on your own computer, or costs a one-time payment instead of a subscription, here is a selected comparison for 2026: Wispr Flow itself, and two alternatives, KeyStow (the app this site sells) and Superwhisper. This is a focused comparison, not an exhaustive list of every dictation tool.

KeyStow: $5 once, with the speech model built in

KeyStow is an offline voice dictation app for Windows and Apple Silicon Mac. It runs OpenAI's Whisper speech models locally, on your own hardware. Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, dictation works without internet too, and KeyStow keeps no audio recording or transcript history.

The default speech model ships inside the installer, so it needs no additional download on first use: install it, grant the usual microphone permission (and the accessibility permission on a Mac), hold Right Ctrl on Windows or Right Option on a Mac, and speak. Text is pasted where your cursor is. You can change the key to Right Shift, Right Alt or another key. Larger Whisper models download once if you pick them, for a different balance of speed and accuracy. KeyStow sits in the system tray, starts automatically at sign-in, and shows a simple dashboard of the words you have dictated.

KeyStow costs $5 once, no subscription and no account, for lifetime access including updates. The source code is open under the MIT license.

Superwhisper: local models free, AI extras paid

Superwhisper's free plan gives you unlimited dictation with local Whisper models, and its own docs say that on the free plan nothing you dictate leaves your device. The Pro plan adds cloud models, AI processing and file transcription, at $8.49 per month, $84.99 per year, or $249.99 for a lifetime license. One license covers Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android.

Which Wispr Flow alternative should you pick?

  • Pick KeyStow if you want offline dictation on Windows or Apple Silicon Mac with the default speech model already inside the installer: no model download on first use, no account, and $5 once covers it for good.
  • Pick Superwhisper if you want a free local option with paid AI extras on top, or you need dictation on your iPhone too.
  • Stay with Wispr Flow if you want one account covering Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android with cloud features, and a monthly or annual plan is fine.

The choice comes down to the fit questions above: where your voice is processed, which devices you use, and whether you prefer a subscription, a free local tier, or one $5 payment.
