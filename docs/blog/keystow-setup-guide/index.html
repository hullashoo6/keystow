How to Set Up KeyStow on Windows or Mac

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

What you need

  • A Windows PC or an Apple Silicon Mac.
  • One download.
  • Five dollars, once. No account, no subscription.

Step 1: Download and install

Go to hullashoo6.github.io/keystow and download the installer for your computer, then run it. The app bundles its Whisper speech model, so there is no second download or separate setup for the speech engine.

Step 2: Grant microphone access

Your operating system will ask for permission to use the microphone. Grant it; the app cannot hear your speech without it. KeyStow processes your voice privately on your own computer and never sends it to the cloud, so this permission stays local.

Step 3: Start it and check it is running

KeyStow runs quietly in the background and can start automatically when you sign in, so it is always there when you need it. Closing its window does not stop it; it keeps listening for your shortcut. To actually exit, quit it explicitly.

Step 4: Learn the one shortcut

The default hold-to-talk key is Right Option on Mac (with a Windows equivalent). Hold it, talk, release: the words type wherever your cursor is. If the default clashes with your habits, open KeyStow's settings and pick a different key. Hold-to-talk also means you dictate exactly when you choose; nothing listens in between.

Step 5: Test it once

1. Open any text field: Notes, Notepad, your email draft.

2. Hold your shortcut key and say a short sentence.

3. Release. Your sentence should appear.

If it does, you are done setting up. Use it everywhere from now on: email, docs, chat, forms.

What you do NOT have to do

  • Create an account or sign in.
  • Enter payment details again. $5 once, lifetime.
  • Worry about your recordings. KeyStow keeps no audio recording or transcript history.
  • Stay online. Dictation works without internet.
