KeyStow turns your voice into text, anywhere on your computer. Press a key, speak naturally, press again - polished words appear right where your cursor is.

Install for WindowsInstall for Mac (Apple Silicon)

Free & open source · Pay once if you love it · No account needed

First install? Windows may show a blue "Windows protected your PC" screen - that's normal for new apps. Click More info, then Run anyway.

On Mac (Apple Silicon): open the dmg, drag KeyStow to Applications, then right-click the app and choose Open -> Open the first time (a normal one-time step for indie apps). Allow Microphone and Accessibility access when macOS asks.

01

Install in one click

Download the installer, run it, and KeyStow sits quietly in your tray. No Python, no setup wizard maze.

02

Press and talk

Press Right Ctrl once, say what you mean, press again. In email, chat, docs - anywhere you can type, you can now speak.

03

Stay private

Transcription runs entirely on your own machine. Your voice never leaves your device. No cloud, no subscription.

Powered by OpenAI's Whisper, running 100% locally. No audio is ever saved or uploaded - no cloud, no account, no telemetry. The only things stored on your computer are your settings and your word counts.
