Voice Dictation for Consultants: Reports, Recommendations and Client Recaps

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

The deliverable is the product

Consulting is thinking, but the invoice references documents: the assessment, the recommendation deck's talking points, the Friday recap email. Most consultants think faster than they type, so the writing step is where engagements stall. Dictation moves the bottleneck back to the thinking, where it belongs.

Speaking a first draft is also a quality check. If a recommendation does not sound convincing when you say it aloud, it will not read convincingly either.

What consultants dictate

  • Findings and observations. Right after a client interview or site walk-through, while the specifics are fresh.
  • Recommendation drafts. Argue the recommendation out loud as if presenting it, then edit the transcript into the report.
  • Client recaps. The two-paragraph "here is where we are" email that keeps a project warm, written in the taxi instead of at 10 PM.
  • Proposals and statements of work. Scope sections have a natural spoken rhythm: what we will do, what you will get, what it depends on.

A note on client confidentiality

Consultants carry client-confidential material by definition. Where your audio is processed matters: a cloud dictation service sends everything you say to a third party. KeyStow processes your voice on your own computer. That removes one exposure, but it does not by itself make any tool compliant with your client's security policy or your engagement letter. Check those terms first; they govern what tools you may use at all.

How KeyStow fits

KeyStow is an offline voice dictation app for Windows and Apple Silicon Mac. Hold Right Ctrl on Windows (Right Option on a Mac), speak, release, and the text is typed where your cursor is. Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, and dictation works without internet too. KeyStow does not keep an audio recording or transcript history.
