Dictation Apps With No Subscription: Your Options in 2026

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

Software rent adds up. A dictation app at $12 to $15 per month costs $144 to $180 every year, for as long as you keep dictating. If you are tired of subscriptions, you do not have to rent your voice typing: several solid dictation apps are free or charge a single one-time payment. Here is the no-subscription landscape for 2026.

Why dictation went subscription, and why it does not have to be

Subscriptions made sense when transcription required expensive cloud servers. It no longer does: open speech models like OpenAI's Whisper run well on an ordinary laptop, so the ongoing server cost that justified monthly billing is gone for basic dictation. The apps below all work without a recurring fee.

KeyStow: $5 once, lifetime access

KeyStow is an offline voice dictation application for Windows and Apple Silicon Mac. Hold a key (Right Ctrl on Windows, Right Option on a Mac by default; changeable), speak, release, and your words are pasted at the cursor in any app. Whisper models run locally, so dictation works with no internet and your voice never leaves your computer. No account, no recordings kept, no monthly plan. The $5 covers lifetime access including updates, and the source code is MIT open source.

Handy: completely free

Handy is a free, open source speech-to-text app for Windows, macOS and Linux. Same basic gesture: press a shortcut, speak, release, text appears. It costs nothing, ever, and offers a choice of Whisper and Parakeet models.

Superwhisper free plan: local dictation at $0

Superwhisper's free plan gives unlimited dictation using local Whisper models on your device, on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android. It is genuinely free for local use; the Pro subscription ($8.49 per month) and $249.99 lifetime license only matter if you want its cloud models and AI extras.

Built-in dictation: already on your computer

  • Windows voice typing (Windows key + H) is free, but Microsoft's support page says it uses online speech recognition powered by Azure and needs an internet connection.
  • Apple Dictation is free on every Mac. Depending on your Mac and settings, requests are processed on the device or sent to Siri servers; Apple's guide shows how to check.

What a subscription actually buys elsewhere

To be fair to the other side: subscription dictation apps like Wispr Flow ($12 to $15 per user per month) pay for cloud transcription, mobile apps, meeting notes and sync across devices. If you use those features daily, the rent may be worth it. If you just want to speak and see text appear on your computer, it is money you do not need to spend.

Choosing

  • Daily dictation on Windows or Mac, private and paid once: KeyStow, $5.
  • Free and open source, with options like transcript history and a custom dictionary: Handy.
  • Free local dictation with an upgrade path: Superwhisper's free plan.
  • Occasional short dictation: the built-in option on your computer.
