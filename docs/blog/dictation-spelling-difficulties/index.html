Speaking Instead of Spelling: Dictation for People Who Find Spelling Hard

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

When the spelling is the wall, not the idea

Plenty of sharp thinkers write slowly because spelling gets in the way. You know exactly what you want to say; you lose it while hunting for which letters go in "necessary." Emails go short, reports go thin, and the idea dies on the way to the keyboard.

Dictation separates the two jobs. Speaking gets the idea down whole; the software spells it; you edit for sense. The writing finally matches the thinking.

A workflow that plays to strengths

  • Speak the whole thought first. Do not stop mid-sentence to fix a word. Finishing the thought matters more than any single correction.
  • Let the tools do their jobs. The dictation app turns speech into correctly spelled text most of the time; your editor or spell-check catches the rest.
  • Read it back by listening. Reading your own text aloud, or having it read to you, catches awkward spots that silent reading skims over.
  • Keep a personal word list. Names, jargon and the words that always come out wrong, noted once and checked every time.

Good habits for anyone

Everything here is just good writing practice: draft first, fix second, verify names and numbers. The difference is that dictation moves the hard part to the end, where it stops blocking the start.

How KeyStow fits
