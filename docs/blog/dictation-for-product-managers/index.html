Voice Dictation for Product Managers: Specs, PRDs and Stakeholder Updates

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

PMs are professional explainers

A product manager's output is mostly prose: PRDs, specs, one-pagers, roadmap updates, Slack threads, launch announcements, feedback summaries. The thinking is the job; the writing is how the thinking ships. And the writing is slow, because typing is slow relative to how fast you can explain a feature out loud, which is what you do in meetings all day.

Where dictation fits the PM week

  • PRDs and specs: talk through the problem, the user, the solution and the edge cases as if pitching the team. The spoken pass surfaces gaps in your own reasoning before engineering finds them.
  • Stakeholder updates: the weekly written update executives actually read is easier to say than to type. Dictate it in your status-meeting voice, then tighten.
  • Feedback and research synthesis: after user calls, speak the themes while they are fresh instead of reconstructing them from notes days later.
  • Meeting follow-ups: decisions and action items, dictated immediately after the meeting while the context is loaded in your head.

The practical start

Pick your lowest-stakes recurring document (the weekly update is perfect) and dictate the next three. By the third, you will have your own sense of where speaking beats typing in your calendar, and it is rarely where you expected.
