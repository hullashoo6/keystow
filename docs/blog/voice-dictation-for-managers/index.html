Voice Dictation for Managers: One-on-One Notes and Performance Reviews

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

The management paper trail is real work

One-on-one notes, feedback write-ups, performance reviews, skip-level summaries: a manager of eight produces a small book of writing every quarter. Most of it is written late, from memory, because the day is meetings. The quality of your reviews and feedback decisions is capped by the quality of those notes.

The fix is timing, not effort. Two minutes of dictation after each one-on-one captures specifics that a review-season memory search never will.

What managers dictate

  • One-on-one notes. Wins, blockers, commitments, moods, right after the conversation ends.
  • Feedback in the moment. Dictate the specific behavior and impact while it is specific, then deliver it in person. Written feedback later quotes your note, not your haze.
  • Review drafts. Twelve weeks of two-minute notes turn the annual review from archaeology into editing.
  • Skip-level and peer summaries. What you heard, what you verified, what you are doing about it.

A note on confidentiality

People notes are among the most sensitive documents a company holds. KeyStow processes your voice on your own computer and keeps no audio recording or transcript history, which avoids sending them to a cloud service. Your company's HR and IT policies still govern where such notes may be taken and stored at all, so check them first.

How KeyStow fits

KeyStow is an offline voice dictation app for Windows and Mac. Hold Right Ctrl on Windows (Right Option on a Mac), speak, release, and the text is pasted where your cursor is, in your notes app, HR system text fields or email. Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, and dictation works without internet too.
