How to Dictate Product Descriptions for Your Online Store

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

Every seller knows the description problem

The supplier's copy is right there, one paste away. It is also on fifty other stores, it reads like a catalog, and it does not answer the question your customer actually has. Unique descriptions convert better and give search engines something only you have. The catch: writing three hundred of them by hand is a month of evenings.

Dictation changes the math. You know your products the way you know a good story. Describing one out loud, the way you would to a customer in a shop, takes two minutes and sounds like you.

A two-minute-per-product workflow

  • Handle or photograph the item first. Specifics come from looking: the weight, the stitching, the smell of the leather.
  • Answer three questions out loud. What is it, who is it for, what is the detail nobody mentions? That is the description.
  • Dictate the specs separately. Sizes, materials, care instructions, in a list. Prose persuades; specs inform.
  • Batch by category. Descriptions for similar items flow faster in one sitting, and your phrasing stays consistent.

The two checks that matter

Read for accuracy: measurements, materials and claims must be exactly right, and "handmade" or "waterproof" are promises, not adjectives. Then check the tone once against your shop's voice. Keep the spoken warmth; cut the "um, basically."
