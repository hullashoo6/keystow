How to Dictate Offline on Windows (No Cloud, No Subscription)

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

Most voice typing on Windows sends your audio to the cloud. Windows' own voice typing (Windows key + H) uses online speech recognition powered by Azure Speech services, and Microsoft's support page says you need an internet connection for it. Cloud dictation tools work the same way: your microphone audio travels to a server, gets transcribed, and comes back as text.

Offline dictation is different. The speech recognition model runs on your PC, your voice never leaves it, and dictation keeps working with no internet at all. Here is how to set it up, and what your options are.

What you need

  • A Windows PC with a working microphone (a laptop's built-in mic is fine).
  • An offline dictation app: one that runs a speech model locally instead of calling a cloud service.
  • No account and no internet connection after the app is installed.

Set up offline dictation with KeyStow

  • Download and install KeyStow. Run the Windows installer. It installs for your user account, so it does not need admin rights.
  • Pick a speech model size. KeyStow runs OpenAI's Whisper models locally. Smaller models are faster, larger ones are more accurate. You can change the model later.
  • Put your cursor where you want text. Any text field works: a document, an email, a chat box, a browser form.
  • Hold Right Ctrl, speak, release. When you release the key, KeyStow transcribes what you said on your PC and pastes it at the cursor. You can change the activation key to Right Shift, Right Alt or another key.
  • Keep working. KeyStow sits in the system tray and starts automatically when you sign in, so dictation is always one key away.

After installation, none of this needs the internet. Unplug, and dictation still works. Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, and KeyStow does not keep an audio recording or transcript history.

Other offline options on Windows

  • Handy is a free, open source speech-to-text app with the same press-talk-release flow. Transcription happens on your computer. It is free (MIT), with Whisper and Parakeet model options, transcript history and a custom dictionary.
  • Superwhisper has a free plan that runs local Whisper models with unlimited dictation. Its paid Pro plan adds cloud models and AI features, which are not offline.
