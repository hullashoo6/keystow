Voice Dictation for Mac Without a Subscription

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

Mac users have plenty of dictation choices, but most of the good ones want a monthly plan. Wispr Flow Pro is $12 to $15 per month. Superwhisper Pro starts at $8.49 per month. If you want dictation on a Mac without a subscription, here are your real options in 2026, from free to $5 once.

Apple's built-in Dictation: free, with limits

Every Mac has Dictation built in (enable it in System Settings, Keyboard). Whether your voice is processed on the device or sent to Siri servers depends on your Mac and settings; Apple's own guide tells you how to check in Keyboard settings. Built-in Dictation is fine for short text, but it has no global hold-to-talk key you can customize, fewer controls, and no way to choose a stronger speech model.

KeyStow: $5 once, offline, built for hold-to-talk

KeyStow is an offline voice dictation application for Windows and Mac. It runs OpenAI's Whisper speech models locally: your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, and dictation works without internet. KeyStow does not keep an audio recording or transcript history.

On a Mac, the default activation key is Right Option (MacBook keyboards have no Right Ctrl), and you can pick a different key. Hold it, speak, release, and the text is pasted at your cursor, in any app. It starts automatically when you sign in. $5 once buys lifetime access including updates, and the code is MIT open source.

Superwhisper free plan: local and unlimited, Mac-first

Superwhisper started on the Mac, and its free plan gives unlimited dictation with local Whisper models on your device. If you later want cloud models, AI processing or file transcription, Pro is $8.49 per month, $84.99 per year, or $249.99 lifetime.

Handy: free and open source

Handy is a free, open source speech-to-text app that also runs on macOS (alongside Windows and Linux). Press a shortcut, speak, release, and the text appears in your text field, processed on your computer. It offers Whisper and Parakeet models, transcript history and a custom dictionary.

Quick comparison for Mac users

If you dictate occasionally, start with Apple's built-in Dictation or Handy. If you dictate every day and want a dedicated hold-to-talk tool that stays private and never charges you again, KeyStow is $5 once.
