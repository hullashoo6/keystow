Wispr Flow Pricing Explained, and a $5 One-Time Alternative

November 24, 2026 · KeyStow · All articles

How much does Wispr Flow actually cost, and what do you get for free? This breakdown uses the prices on Wispr's own pricing page, checked November 24, 2026, and compares them with one-time and free alternatives.

What Wispr Flow costs

  • Free plan, $0. Dictation on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android with a weekly usage limit. Wispr's FAQ says the free plan includes "generous weekly usage" and needs no credit card.
  • Flow Pro, $15 per user per month billed monthly, or $12 per user per month billed annually. Pro removes the usage limits and adds team management features; the Notetaker is included.

Two more things to know. First, Wispr's privacy page says transcription always happens in the cloud, on every plan including the free one. Second, Pro is a subscription: it costs money every month for as long as you use it.

What that adds up to

Simple arithmetic from the listed prices, before any discounts:

Three years of Flow Pro costs between $432 and $540. KeyStow costs $5 once, for lifetime access including updates.

The $5 one-time alternative: KeyStow

KeyStow is an offline voice dictation application for Windows and Mac, and it takes the opposite approach on both price and privacy. You pay $5 once, and the speech recognition (OpenAI's Whisper models) runs locally on your computer. Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to the cloud, dictation works without internet, and KeyStow does not keep an audio recording or transcript history. There is no account and no monthly plan.

What you give up: KeyStow has no iPhone or Android app, no meeting notetaker, and no cloud sync between devices. If you need those, Wispr Flow's subscription is the price of having them.

Other ways to pay less

  • Stay on Wispr Flow's free plan if the weekly usage limit covers your dictation.
  • Superwhisper's free plan gives unlimited dictation with local Whisper models on your device; its Pro plan starts at $8.49 per month, and a lifetime license is $249.99.
  • Handy is completely free and open source, on Windows, macOS and Linux, with local processing.

The short version

Wispr Flow is a good product with a real ongoing cost: $12 to $15 per month, and your audio goes to the cloud. If you want dictation without either of those, KeyStow does the core job, offline, for $5 once.
