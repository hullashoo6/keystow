Opening KeyStow for the first time on a Mac
===========================================

KeyStow is not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm it once,
the first time you open it. After that it opens like any other app.

1. Drag KeyStow onto the Applications folder in this window.

2. Open your Applications folder and double-click KeyStow.
   macOS says it could not verify KeyStow. Click Done.

3. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the
   Security section. Next to the message about KeyStow being blocked,
   click Open Anyway, then confirm with your password or Touch ID.
   (On macOS 14 you can instead Control-click KeyStow in Applications,
   choose Open, then Open again.)

4. KeyStow opens. It needs two permissions, both under System Settings >
   Privacy & Security:
   - Microphone: macOS asks the first time you dictate. Click Allow.
   - Accessibility, for the shortcut and for pasting: KeyStow asks when it
     starts. Click Open System Settings, switch KeyStow on, then quit and
     reopen KeyStow.

5. Click into any text box, hold Right Option, say a sentence and let go.
   Your words appear where the cursor is.

Your voice is processed privately on your own computer and never sent to
the cloud. Dictation works without internet too.

Needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later.
